### [P2] Expired access on the OAuth row still has no consequence

When OAuth is ready, the status is `Intervals access expired` in mist, then Reconnect and Disconnect, and the helper is empty (`src/components/ConnectedApps.astro:56-59`). Today already says what resumes.

**Why it matters:** The runner arrives from Reconnect and gets a status word, not the reason they tapped it.

**Fix:** Repeat that imports and planned workouts stay stopped until they reconnect.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable clarify`

### [P2] A thrown intervals post still replaces Settings

`handleSettingsPost` returns the unavailable sentence with status 403 on an unexpected throw (`src/lib/training.ts:2173`). Settings writes that as `text/plain` (`src/pages/settings.astro:63-67`). Sync when OAuth and encryption are both off does the same (`src/lib/training.ts:2215`). The half-filled key form does not.

**Why it matters:** The shell, the nav, and the way back disappear. The sentence is friendly. The page is not.

**Fix:** Paint that sentence in the row alert. Keep a naked 403 for a cross-site request.

**Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`
